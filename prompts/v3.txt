Role: Customer support classifier.
Task: Identify category and priority.
Constraints: Use only Billing, Technical, Account and Low, Medium, High.
Output format: JSON object with category and priority only.
Examples:
Ticket: The website is unavailable for everyone.
Answer: {"category":"Technical","priority":"High"}
Ticket: Please send my purchase receipt.
Answer: {"category":"Billing","priority":"Low"}
Ticket: Update the email on my profile.
Answer: {"category":"Account","priority":"Low"}
